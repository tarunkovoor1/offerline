Offerline

A macOS app that helps high school athletes receive an offer at the finish line. It keeps college coaching contacts, resumes, email templates, and notes in one place, and lets you send a personalized email to a coach in a couple of clicks. 


The problem:
Getting recruited means contacting coaches at many colleges. Each school has several coaches; you send different versions of your athletic resume, and you need to remember what was said to whom. Spreadsheets and scattered email drafts get messy quickly. RecruitReady replaces them with one dashboard.

Features:
- Dashboard: one tile per college showing its contacts, the resume and email draft assigned to it, and notes.
- College contacts: stores each college's name, division, and up to five coaches with title, email and phone.
- Resume manager: import PDF, RTF or Word resumes and preview them in the app with PDFKit.
- Email drafts: reusable templates with To / CC / BCC / Subject / Body fields.
- One-click outreach: combines a college's coach contacts, a chosen draft and a resume into a ready-to-send email in the user's mail client, with the resume attached.
- Notes for each college: a rich-text (RTF) notes editor for logging conversations and next steps.
